SUBJECT: UPCOMING RECRUITMENT DRIVE - APEX GLOBAL TECHNOLOGIES

1. COMPANY OVERVIEW:
Apex Global Technologies is a premier cloud engineering, AI platforms, and enterprise 
infrastructure solutions provider headquartered in Bengaluru with global delivery centers 
across North America, Europe, and Asia-Pacific.

2. ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA:
- Target Degree: B.Tech / B.E. / M.Tech
- Eligible Branches: Computer Science & Engineering (CSE), Information Technology (IT), 
  Electronics & Communication Engineering (ECE), and Data Science.
- Academic Threshold: Minimum Cumulative Grade Point Average (CGPA) of 7.50 with no active backlogs.
- Minimum 10th and 12th / Diploma aggregate of 70.0% or above.
- Maximum gap in academic study: 1 Year (duly justified).

3. COMPENSATION & ROLE DESIGNATION:
- Designation: Associate Cloud Solutions Engineer / Graduate Software Engineer
- Annual Cost to Company (CTC): INR 12,00,000/- Per Annum (Fixed: 10 LPA + Performance Bonus: 2 LPA)
- Work Location: Bengaluru / Hyderabad / Pune (Hybrid model)
- Probation / Internship Period: 6 Months stipend @ INR 35,000/- per month.

4. SELECTION PROCEDURE:
- Stage 1: Online Technical Assessment (Data Structures, Algorithms, SQL, and CS Core Fundamentals) - 90 Minutes.
- Stage 2: Coding Hackathon / Problem Solving Evaluation (2 Complex algorithmic challenges) - 60 Minutes.
- Stage 3: Technical Interview Round 1 & Round 2 (System Architecture, Projects, Operating Systems).
- Stage 4: Leadership & HR Behavioral Assessment.

5. SCHEDULE & KEY DATES:
- Student Registration Deadline: October 20, 2025 at 17:00 IST.
- Online Assessment Date: October 24, 2025 (Slot timings to be conveyed via SMS/Email).
- Campus Interview Rounds: October 28 - October 29, 2025.

6. MANDATORY INSTRUCTIONS:
- Formal western attire is mandatory for all interview rounds.
- Carry 3 updated hard copies of resume along with college identity card.
- Late submissions will not be entertained under any circumstances.
